# Candid assessment

The substantive work is source selection and entity-scope reconciliation, followed by an inspectable financing constraint system. The model distinguishes solvable rollover requirements from cases where even full refinancing cannot preserve a cash floor. It retains a severe historical challenge, an explicit recovery alternative and unfavorable results rather than optimizing a headline.

This is a credible research / finance diligence prototype, not a complete corporate treasury model. The 2025 annual base, six selected issuers, incomplete facility / covenant data and assumed capex prevent current financing recommendations. The independent solver verifies the implemented mathematics; it cannot prove the policy assumptions or completeness of disclosures. The work does not establish consulting tenure, professional licensing or investment skill.

To advance beyond a portfolio prototype, reconcile all debt instruments and subsequent transactions, verify actual committed liquidity / covenants, incorporate property cash requirements and tax constraints, review scope choices with treasury staff, and rerun decision sensitivities with documented management policies. Preserve the present source snapshot as the historical research version.
