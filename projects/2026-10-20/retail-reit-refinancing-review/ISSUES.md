# Open evidence and adoption issues

- Annual audited base is dated December 2025. Obtain current cash, debt drawings, acquisitions and subsequent financing before any current decision.
- No lender commitment, covenant headroom, borrowing-base test, secured-asset capacity or actual available revolver is modeled. Excluding these sources can overstate a modeled cash gap.
- Maintenance capex and cash floor are deliberately simple policy assumptions. Property-level capex, tenant improvements, leasing commissions, redevelopment and restricted cash needs can materially change the answer.
- Fixed distributions avoid inventing management flexibility. Actual REIT tax-distribution requirements, preferred obligations and discretionary reductions need separate review.
- Debt is assumed refinanced beyond the horizon. Financing fees, base rate reset mechanics, hedges and maturity within the new debt need instrument-level diligence.
- Sparse annual CFO history is descriptive, not a probability distribution. A historically observed one-year decline does not justify five years of persistence; the recovery sensitivity must accompany the severe case.
- FRT's cash-return scope includes minority / redemption flows; issuer distribution comparability remains imperfect. KIM excluded JV debt may still matter economically through guarantees or support obligations.
- Incremental liquidity gaps exclude the interest / fees of a new facility and cannot be quoted as actual financing needs.
- No treasury user, lender, physical mobile device or screen-reader acceptance is claimed. CI browser evidence covers the stated engines / emulated viewport sizes only.
