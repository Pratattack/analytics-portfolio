# Candid assessment

This is a substantive finance/actuarial diligence research prototype. Its depth is original dimensional disclosure extraction, an independently reconciled source trail, explicit model exclusions, matched economic scopes, a simple benchmark and the decision to withhold capital-release approval despite a favorable-looking default model gap.

The 14.54% masked-diagonal WAPE versus 44.50% for the repeat-increment benchmark does not establish future forecasting accuracy. The default tail is not validated, the oldest ages are sparse, several source patterns violate the model's assumptions, and the historical information set has not been reconstructed. The sensitivity envelope is not a confidence interval. It is not an actuarial opinion or an insurance investment recommendation.

What Prathamesh should defend: one original context and scaled number, a hand-computed factor, why nil differs from zero, why a cumulative loss table is not an annual cash-flow table, how the tail changes the projected ultimate, why excluded data remain visible and what independent actuarial evidence would change the current no-release decision.

The project does not claim a commissioned engagement, statutory-capital expertise, 8–10 years of employment, production certification or guaranteed recruiter outcomes.
