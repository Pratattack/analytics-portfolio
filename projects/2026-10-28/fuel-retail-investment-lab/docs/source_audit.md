# Source audit and decision implications

## Document definitions

F01 is an anonymized April 2026 investment decision memo supplied by the owner. Page references follow the original seven-page PDF, whose last page is blank. R01 is an anonymized April 2026 risk assessment; page references follow an eleven-page LibreOffice render and may differ in Word. The underlying market/feasibility report referenced in both documents was not supplied. Neither source's estimates are independently verified operating results.

## Audited calculations

F01 p.1 lists nonfuel categories that sum exactly to $124,227, $138,031 and $151,834 in low, average and high cases. The average case comprises $87,389 store sales, $37,359 lottery, $11,623 deli and $1,660 pizza. The model assumes these categories do not overlap; the source does not establish that assumption. These totals are receipts estimates, not a profit calculation. Construction costs, expense detail, financing and investment returns are not supplied.

The fuel row on F01 p.1 is labeled as a gallons-times-margin calculation but supplies gallons only. At 10,200 daily vehicles, 3% capture and 365/12 days, 44,154 monthly gallons imply 4.74 gallons per captured transaction. If only half of traffic is eligible, the equivalent purchase is 9.49 gallons. This reverse calculation exposes a denominator assumption; it does not prove capture or purchase size.

Lottery is 27.07% of the stated average nonfuel receipts. The model interprets the reported value as ticket sales rather than commission. At the model's 5% commission assumption, retailer commission is $1,867.95; $35,491.05 is not retained by the retailer. Texas Comptroller E01 confirms a statutory minimum of 5%, not the terms of an actual project contract. Bonuses are excluded. The interpretation must be confirmed before operational use.

F01 p.2's three populations sum to 4,355 and its Hispanic counts sum to 1,353, or 31.07%. Population-weighting the three median household income estimates gives $62,895, but it does not recover the combined household income distribution or establish a trade-area median. No ACS vintage, margins of error or overlap/coverage methodology is supplied. Statements that income mix provides a natural hedge or that a demographic group is underserved are untested claims, not inputs to cash flow.

F01 pp.2-3 claim plausible growth, capture and competitor differentiation without supporting observations. Its p.4 conditional-proceed recommendation is an opinion. The stated approximate site acreage and program are not a civil site plan. Zoning, access, approval times and competitor assertions are not treated as verified present conditions.

## Risk-register consistency

R01 p.2 states low 1-7, medium 8-14, high 15-19 and critical 20-25. Recomputing the 31 register rows on pp.3-6 produces one critical, six high, eighteen medium and six low ratings under those rules. Four supplied labels conflict:

- Site fit: 4 by 5 = 20, critical rather than high.
- Access permit delay/denial: 3 by 5 = 15, high rather than medium.
- POS outage: 2 by 4 = 8, medium rather than low.
- Post-opening tank leak: 2 by 5 = 10, medium rather than low.

R01's p.7 heat map also differs from its register: site fit is placed at 15 instead of 20, and the access-permit label appears at 12 instead of 15. Domain scores on pp.1-2 have no defined aggregation method. The project therefore does not average them, claim calibrated risk probabilities or assign event distributions for Monte Carlo simulation.

R01 pp.10-11 reduce residual risk after planned controls without evidence of implementation or measured effects. These remain assertions. The project retains unresolved evidence gates instead of marking risk low because an action is scheduled.

## External checks and unresolved legal/technical claims

E01 is the Texas Comptroller's current expenditure code 7260 page, revised 20 August 2025 and accessed 28 October 2026. It supports the at-least-5% commission statement and separates retailer bonuses. It does not verify an individual retailer contract.

E02 is TCEQ RG-475b, revised January 2024 and retrieved 28 October 2026. Pages 1-2 describe licensed installation, notification and registration responsibilities. Page 3 lists several permitted tank materials. Page 4 explains double-wall/secondary-containment requirements for newer systems. R01 p.9's claim that fiberglass specifically is required is overbroad; fiberglass is one permitted material, not the only one. The guidance does not establish site-specific compliance, approval, environmental condition or a fixed permit completion date.

E03 is TxDOT's official traffic-count access page, retrieved 28 October 2026. It identifies count-map and STARS sources. The project has not matched a station, direction and year to the private parcel, so it does not replace the report's assumption with a purported verified local count. No private address is embedded in requests or public source snapshots.

## Public release scope

Only sanitized numerical extracts, generic risk descriptions and original analysis are prepared for release. Personal names, business names from the reports, exact address, city, postal/parcel identifiers, block-group IDs and the original attachments are excluded. Authorization to use the contents does not mean the project author wrote the source reports. Public case-study text describes an independent re-analysis, with hypothetical cost and financing inputs.
