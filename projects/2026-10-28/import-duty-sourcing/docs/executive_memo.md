# Import duty and sourcing tradeoffs

Transformers, converters and inductors: 8.57% to 12.61%; Electric accumulators: 14.11% to 16.97%; Semiconductor devices: 7.23% to 7.27%. These are historical calculated-duty ratios, not legal tariff schedules or causal estimates.

## Decision use
Prioritize categories for quote and compliance investigation; do not select a supplier on aggregate historical duty ratios. Separate within-cell rate and composition changes, and inspect excluded value before generalizing.

## Limits
Calculated duties are not proof of cash collected, final tariff incidence or supplier cost. HTS10/origin cells can still conceal quality, supplier and timing changes. Identical code descriptions are a conservative comparability screen, not a legal concordance. Balanced-panel results exclude entry/exit and report their coverage. Countries are not suppliers. No quote, product interchangeability, switching feasibility, capacity, delivery time, product-origin eligibility or future tariff rate is established. Retrieved historical files may contain revisions; this is not a point-in-time policy backtest.
