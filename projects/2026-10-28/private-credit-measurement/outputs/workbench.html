Interpret the boundary

Above 100% means marking the entire measured U.S. private-loan book would not reach the selected screen under the other fixed assumptions. A negative boundary means the screen is already breached after the equity mark. The tool does not extrapolate a feasible loss beyond 100%. CLOs are excluded because the table does not separately report equity. Cash is not assumed to be unencumbered or available for redemptions.

The observations are 2025 Q4 balances published in October 2026, not current live holdings. Aggregate populations conceal individual fund dispersion. Private loans may include some syndicated loans; foreign positions use estimation assumptions. Missing cells remain null. CLO equity is not separately reported, so CLOs are excluded from equity-buffer comparisons. No redemption, liquidity runway, default probability, recovery forecast, mark-to-market timing or institution-level contagion is inferred.
