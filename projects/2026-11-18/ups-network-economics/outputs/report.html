Decision thresholds

At 35% incremental contribution, a 2.48% domestic revenue decline would erase the $129 million year-over-year Q3 adjusted improvement. The investment hurdle instead compares incremental cash flow with holding the latest network unchanged. It assumes $150 million of upfront cash cost and a four-quarter ramp.

Stage the decision

Start with a limited lane pilot, verify service and contribution on affected accounts, and include stranded cost and incremental capex in the cash case. Advance only if measured net savings clear the hurdle under a credible leakage stress. The eight-quarter screen holds the Q3 revenue base flat and excludes taxes, working capital, seasonality and terminal value. The cash cost is an assumption separate from UPS’s reported accounting charges.
