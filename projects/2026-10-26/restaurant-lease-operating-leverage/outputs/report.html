Restaurant Lease & Operating Leverage

A finance-committee review of earnings resilience and conditional response capacity. Six issuers; dated fiscal-year 2025 base.

Decision brief

The retained panel contains 32 issuer-years. Latest annual operating lease cost totals $1.089B, compared with $1.099B of reported operating lease payments. These are different accounting measures.

Prioritize operational diligence where the conditional response frontier is insufficient. 32 of 216 assumed scenarios remain infeasible; 33 are feasible only when both response levers are available. No actual turnaround benefits are measured.

How the decision is bounded

Earnings after stress = reported EBIT − revenue × decline × assumed contribution margin − lease expense × incremental escalation. Base rent is already in EBIT and is not deducted again. The response frontier minimizes assumed resource cost of capped savings and recovered sales; it does not optimize layoffs, closures or rent defaults.

Annual fiscal ends differ; coverage is uneven. EBIT includes depreciation and lease expense, impairments and other operating items. Lease-cost scope and cash-payment scope can differ, and consolidation/acquisitions change comparability. No store-level contribution margins, implementation costs, elasticities, landlord concessions, current liquidity or lender covenants are observed. The response weights and capacity caps are assumed. A mathematical optimum does not establish that management can execute it.
