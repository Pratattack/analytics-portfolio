Interpretation limits

Annual fiscal ends differ; coverage is uneven. EBIT includes depreciation and lease expense, impairments and other operating items. Lease-cost scope and cash-payment scope can differ, and consolidation/acquisitions change comparability. No store-level contribution margins, implementation costs, elasticities, landlord concessions, current liquidity or lender covenants are observed. The response weights and capacity caps are assumed. A mathematical optimum does not establish that management can execute it.
