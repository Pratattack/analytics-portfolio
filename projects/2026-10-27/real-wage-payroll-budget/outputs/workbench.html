What hourly raise would meet a weekly pay target?

Change the assumptions and see the required hourly pay adjustment.

Hypothetical assumptions. This calculator does not predict inflation or recommend a pay decision. Employee count, benefits, overtime, turnover and employer taxes are outside the calculation.

Why the answer changes

If hours decline, a larger hourly increase is needed to reach the same real weekly pay target. Required hourly change = (1 + inflation) × (1 + real weekly target) ÷ (1 + hours change) − 1. This multiplicative identity avoids adding percentages as if compounding did not matter.
