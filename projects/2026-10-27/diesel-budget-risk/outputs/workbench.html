What would a diesel shock do to your budget?

Choose a volume, quote, reserve and price stress. Every company-level input is hypothetical.

Hypothetical budget scenario. Default price $6.352/gallon is observed on 2026-10-26. The default reserve 7.66% uses the frozen historical rule and completed blocks only. It is not a forecast or a guaranteed 90% protection level.

Keep the assumptions visible

Base cost = quote × gallons. Budget = base cost × (1 + reserve). Stress cost = base cost × (1 + assumed realized price change). Shortfall = max(0, stress cost − budget). Equal weekly volumes are assumed. Volume changes, tax treatment, delivery charges, contracts and hedges require a separate model.
