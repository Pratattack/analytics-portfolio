# Candid assessment

This is an advanced **financial diligence research prototype**, not corporate ROIC or a live rail budget. The strongest parts are scope-aware source lineage, reconciling original filing cells, refusing to blend stale periods, exposing finance-lease asset changes, independently checked cash accounting and a workbench that makes tradeoffs visible.

It is a deliberately narrow three-issuer study with 32 admitted annual records. Exact arithmetic and a polished interface do not turn book-asset proxies into project returns or establish a safe maintenance budget. FY2025 cash subtotals need current treasury, transaction and operating evidence before action. Describe it as an independent finance case; do not claim measured savings, professional tenure, a current credit conclusion or guaranteed recruiter outcomes.
