# Open evidence issues

1. **Blocking adoption:** current cash plan, maintenance/growth split, safe minimum reinvestment, merger terms, financing policy and liquidity floors unavailable.
2. **Accounting comparability:** UNP productive assets become finance-lease-inclusive; net/gross book values differ from replacement cost and corporate invested capital.
3. **Vintage:** coherent latest base is FY2025, not current 2026 cash needs. Only original-current annual facts enter the analysis; later revisions remain visible.
4. **Interpretation:** a cash subtotal gap does not identify debt-funded repurchases. No omitted cash source/use is assumed zero. GAAP income is not normalized for incident costs or disposal gains.
5. **Coverage:** 32 annual records, three issuers and nonrandom missing history do not support sector-wide causal or statistical claims.
6. **Delivery limits:** local/offline tool; browser acceptance does not establish lender, engineering, safety or stakeholder validation.
