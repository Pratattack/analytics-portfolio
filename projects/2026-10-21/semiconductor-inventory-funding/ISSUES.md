# Open evidence issues

1. **Blocking promotion:** candidate point forecasts are worse than the last-value baseline; do not retune on the test period.
2. **Blocking current cohort use:** LRCX restructuring-excluded quarterly costs and COHU tax-inclusive revenue need a separately defined, source-reconciled cohort. Do not map them into the current definition without changing the study.
3. **Statistical limits:** only five test issuer clusters, sector dependence, missing early interval scores and no exchangeability guarantee. Forty-seven outcomes are not 47 independent firms.
4. **Business limits:** no SKU aging, purchase commitments, supplier terms, lead times, customer acceptance schedules, backlog or service-level consequences.
5. **Vintage:** today's API aggregates original-current-period facts; no immutable historical API releases or original press-release timing is reconstructed. Later revisions are visible.
6. **Delivery limits:** local/offline tool; browser acceptance does not validate procurement decisions, physical devices or assistive technology.
