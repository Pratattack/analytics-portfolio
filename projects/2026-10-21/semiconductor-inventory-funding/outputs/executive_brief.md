# Executive decision brief — semiconductor equipment inventory

**Decision:** retain the last-disclosed-balance forecasting baseline. Do not promote the candidate ridge model or make procurement/funding commitments from this screen.

## Observed evidence

The audited panel contains 332 coherent issuer-quarters, with 1,160 source operands. On 47 later outcomes (2024–2026 target periods, five issuers), candidate WAPE is 3.025%, versus 2.624% for the last balance and 6.908% for the seasonal balance. The approximate five-issuer bootstrap interval for baseline-minus-model MAE is −$28.61M to −$1.25M; the candidate is worse, not better. Interpret the interval cautiously with only five dependent sector histories.

After enforcing model-selection information availability before calibration, nominal 90% bands cover 86.11% of 36 eligible test outcomes. Eleven early test cases lack enough prior scores and remain in point metrics. There is no theoretical coverage guarantee under these dependent, expanding-refit data.

## Source and funding implications

Only four issuers have coherent current inputs: AMAT, KLAC, ONTO and TER, with admitted inventory totaling $10.995B. The full six-company universe is not a complete current industry sample. Recent LRCX quarters disclose restructuring-excluded custom cost; COHU's recent revenue uses an assessed-tax-inclusive definition. Those series cannot be silently substituted. Their historical rows are visible but blocked from current scenarios.

Inventory exposure is not a procurement cash forecast. Cost balance, obsolescence, payment terms, customer acceptance, lead times and backlog all change the financial implication. A buffer/carriage-cost example is an assumption, not savings. The default workbench therefore uses the last balance; the rejected candidate can be explored only as a diagnostic.

## Recommended sequence and tradeoffs

1. Preserve the baseline and the frozen test result rather than tuning again to make it look positive.
2. Rebuild a consistently defined expanded cohort from original cost/revenue disclosures and document the transition before new validation.
3. Ask procurement for SKU aging, commitments, terms and service constraints; agree a business loss function for underfunding versus excess stock.
4. Reserve a genuinely future period for a revised design and validate jointly with treasury. The current historical test has already been used.

This trades a potentially more responsive model for a simpler, defensible baseline. There is no causal estimate of inventory reductions or authorization to release working capital.
