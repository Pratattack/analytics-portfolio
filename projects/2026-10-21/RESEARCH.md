The modeled base remains the coherent 2025 annual filings, rather than mixing YTD cash flows and annual capex. This screen cannot replace current treasury plans or merger-related analysis.

## Data access and reuse

[SEC EDGAR API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) states that Company Facts aggregates standard-taxonomy, entity-wide facts. Custom tags and dimensional segment data are outside its coverage. The projects explicitly audit that boundary. [SEC developer resources](https://www.sec.gov/developer) describe automated-access expectations. Company Facts snapshots, accession identifiers, filing metadata and extracted numerical inline-XBRL cells are pinned locally with SHA-256 manifests. Full issuer-authored filing narratives are not redistributed; original HTML hashes and source URLs are retained. Issuer materials retain their owners' rights. Numerical observations and our original code are kept distinct; no SEC or issuer endorsement is implied.
