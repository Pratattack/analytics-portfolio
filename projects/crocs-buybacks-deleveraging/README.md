# Crocs: Can it fund buybacks while reducing debt?

**Research cutoff:** November 2, 2026. **Quote date:** October 30, 2026. **Model status:** Scenario analysis, not company guidance.

## The decision
Can Crocs fund meaningful repurchases and repay debt at the same time without confusing share-count-driven EPS growth with genuine operating improvement?

## Base-case result
The first forecast period assumes approximately **$801m** in operating cash flow and **$80m** in capital spending. The base case supports **$600m** in modeled repurchases and about **$134m** in additional debt repayment after maintaining a $150m cash reserve.

## Approach
Five-year integrated three-statement forecast; brand-level contribution assumptions; capital-allocation waterfall; DCF, reverse valuation and sensitivity scenarios; Python validation and SQL historical reconciliation.

## Decision relevance
Repurchases and deleveraging compete for cash. Any buyback plan should be conditional on sustainable brand cash generation, not on apparent EPS accretion alone.

## Caveats
Crocs and HEYDUDE contribution margins are analyst assumptions, not disclosed cash margins. At fixed WACC and terminal growth, the operating scenarios produce DCF per-share values of approximately $71 to $213; this is a scenario range, not a confidence interval or price target.
