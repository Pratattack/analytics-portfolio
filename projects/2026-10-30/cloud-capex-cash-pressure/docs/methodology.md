# Cash-flow scope and normalization

The primary panel contains Microsoft, Alphabet and Meta. Two USD cash-flow tags are required from a shared accession for each period: NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities and PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment. Companyfacts records are restricted to 10-K/10-Q filings through October 30, 2026. Selection for a current or annual period is within 120 days of period end, avoiding replacement of a current disclosure by a much later comparative. Required prior-YTD facts must be in the selected current accession; they are not subject to the current-period filing lag rule.

At each calendar quarter end, interim trailing-twelve-month cash = previous fiscal annual + current fiscal YTD − same-span prior YTD. At fiscal year end, use the annual amount directly. This aligns Microsoft’s June fiscal year with December-year peers without mixing unequal YTD spans. Every contribution carries its sign, start, end, unit, filed date and accession. Missing pairs would be quarantined, not filled; all 93 attempted issuer-quarter observations pass in this release.

The latest 18 numeric inputs, comprising annual, current-YTD and prior-YTD facts for three issuers and two metrics, are reconciled to the original filing contexts and inline-XBRL scale. The extracted audit cells retain context identifiers, raw numeric text, values, original URLs and full-file SHA-256 hashes. Full issuer filings are not bundled; only the relevant numerical extracts and source metadata are retained. Full companyfacts snapshots are represented by the selected tag dictionaries; the original full-download hashes are separate from the included-input hashes. Source refreshes go into a separate cache and never silently replace the pinned inputs.

Amazon is reviewed but excluded from the primary PPE comparison because its current cash acquisitions use PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets. No broader cash definition is substituted merely to fill a peer table. Property-purchase cash is not total AI capex, excludes noncash financing and cannot establish AI profitability. Cash after these purchases is not cash on hand or a full free-cash-flow definition.

The scenario is an explicit annual flow identity: retained = CFO × (1 − decline) − cash PPE × (1 + growth) − other assumed uses. Reverse boundaries solve this identity at an assumed retained-cash target. Negative boundary values indicate that growth cannot fit or that CFO must rise. No probability, investment return or causal effect is estimated.
