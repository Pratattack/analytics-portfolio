# When does infrastructure spending squeeze operating cash?

Alphabet’s property spending runs $4.804 billion ahead of operating cash in the first nine months of 2026, while the trailing-year residual stays positive. The window changes the funding story.

## Finding
Alphabet’s January-September 2026 cash property purchases exceed operating cash by $4.804 billion. Over October 2025-September 2026, operating cash after those purchases remains positive at $19.747 billion. Both statements can be true. A short-window deficit alone does not establish a funding crisis.

## Decision use
Align the cash-flow window before evaluating an investment headline. Then challenge how much spending growth or operating-cash decline fits a specified retained-cash target. Obtain a complete forecast of acquisitions, shareholder distributions, financing and investment commitments before making a funding decision.

## Method
Reconcile 93 trailing-year observations from paired operating-cash and property-purchase facts. Interim TTM equals annual cash flow plus current YTD minus prior YTD; both YTD terms come from one accession. Audit 18 current and annual numeric cells to original filings and reproduce all totals in SQL. Reverse-solve spending and CFO boundaries under explicit assumptions.

## Limits
Company-wide cash purchases are not AI capex or total investment commitments. Noncash lease additions are outside the core measure. Fiscal YTD spans differ and are not ranked as equal windows. Cash after property purchases is before financing and other uses. Amazon’s broader productive-assets tag is excluded rather than silently substituted. No AI returns, stock valuation, liquidity conclusion or achieved business impact is claimed.
