# Currency conventions, netting and policy evaluation

EUR and GBP series quote dollars per foreign unit. JPY, CAD and MXN quote foreign units per dollar and are inverted before exposure returns are calculated. Daily observations are filtered explicitly to January 2015-October 2026 because the downloaded response includes earlier history despite requested bounds. Missing quotes are never forward-filled. Use the final common quote in each completed month; omit partial October from fitting and holdout evaluation, while showing the latest daily context separately. Twenty-five raw quoted values reconcile to the separate October 26 H.10 release table.

Reference foreign-currency amounts are expressed as USD equivalents at a base spot and held fixed for the replay. Positive net exposure denotes a payable; a currency appreciation raises its dollar cost. Receivables have the opposite sign. Same-currency, same-date payment and receipt offsets reduce the transaction base from 17.2m gross to 6.8m absolute net dollars. This is a hypothetical basket and settlement feasibility is assumed. Cross-currency exposures are not mechanically netted into one legally settleable currency.

For monthly spot return vector r, net exposure vector x and hedge fractions h, scenario adverse cash cost is sum[r_i × x_i × (1−h_i)] + b × sum[abs(x_i) × h_i], with assumed per-horizon charge b = 15/10,000. The covered leg is a budget-lock proxy at base spot, not a quoted forward. It omits interest-rate differentials, basis, collateral and execution details.

Fit h using completed-month returns through December 2022. Minimize z + mean(u)/(1−alpha), subject to u >= scenario_cost − z, u >= 0, 0 <= h <= 1 and sum(abs(x) × h) <= 50% × sum(abs(x)). Alpha = 0.9 is prespecified. The resulting five hedge fractions are frozen for January 2023-September 2026, with no holdout selection. Compare no hedge and uniform 50% under the same assumptions. Cost/cap sensitivities are reported, not used to choose a winning holdout configuration.

Empirical CVaR is evaluated by minimizing the threshold-plus-excess expression, including fractional tail mass. It is not a prediction interval, probability of loss or guaranteed future coverage. Only 45 holdout months and roughly 4.5 months of tail mass are available. The source history is current and may be revised, so this is not a point-in-time trading backtest. A lower holdout diagnostic does not establish statistical superiority or operational readiness. JavaScript independently replays the cash identity and empirical tail metric; optimization remains in reproducible Python.
