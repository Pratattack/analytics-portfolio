# Evidence and model boundaries

Seven prespecified seasonally adjusted national industry series: total private, construction, manufacturing, retail, professional and business services, private education and health services, and leisure and hospitality. Total private overlaps the other industries and must not be added to them. Retain monthly observations from January 2015 through August 2026 for job openings, hires and total separations, in levels (thousands) and rates (percent). Do not treat monthly openings as a flow or hires/openings as a fill probability.

The latest 12-month arithmetic mean of monthly separation rates is a planning proxy, not BLS’s official employment-weighted annual rate and not a firm’s employee-level exit probability. Monthly recurrence N[t+1] = (1−s)N[t] + h assumes exits on opening headcount and hiring at month end. The geometric-sum solution gives constant h; require h >= 0 and flag targets below the natural-attrition zero-hire boundary. Fractional staff and hires are continuous planning averages.

Forecast diagnostic: candidates last month, trailing 3-month mean, trailing 12-month mean, and prior-year corresponding three-month mean. Target is the next three-month mean of the published separation rate. Validation target windows end by December 2024, and all start in 2023 or later. Holdout targets begin in January 2025 or later and end no later than August 2026. Drop boundary-straddling windows. Select the lowest validation MAE with lexical tie-break; report holdout MAE and bias against last-month predictions. Numerical differences under 1e-10 percentage points are not wins. Targets overlap, industries overlap, history is revised, and seasonal adjustment uses subsequent observations, so do not claim a real-time backtest, independent trials, statistical significance or prediction intervals.

BLS uses 2022 NAICS from January 2023. We preserve the supplied current classifications and avoid claiming that these broad aggregates identify firm-level structural change. Preliminary footnotes are retained. Raw file dates, retrieval dates and complete-file hashes are distinct from observation months. The separate release-table extract audits levels and rates in August 2025, July 2026 and August 2026. SQL checks the latest 12-month proxy independently.
