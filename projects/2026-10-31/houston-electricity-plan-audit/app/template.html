04 / Evidence and judgment

What this comparison can establish.

Data, audit and scope

Seven selected residential labels from four providers. The selection is purposive, not a representative market sample. Each published rate at 500, 1,000 and 2,000 kWh was reconstructed from charge components and checked against a tolerance of 0.05 cents per kWh for rounding. Two Discount Power contracts share a price function but differ in duration and termination fees.

Monthly usage thresholds are evaluated directly. No interpolation is used to estimate prices between disclosure points. The normalized comparison replaces delivery charges only, using $4.90 per cycle plus 6.5333 cents per kWh from the provider table dated 4 October 2026.

What is excluded, and why?

Time-of-use and free-hours plans need interval consumption data. Solar buyback offers need export data and eligibility checks. These are excluded from the main rankings. An older OhmConnect label includes unquantifiable pass-through charges and is not treated as a fully reconstructed bill. Other candidate documents could not be retrieved completely.

Taxes, deposits, late payments, optional add-ons, local special charges, uncertain pass-throughs and nonrecurring charges are excluded. Termination fees are documented below but not added unless a switch actually occurs. No fees are waived by this model.

Historical labels and contract risk

September, August and April documents are historical observations. Substituting October delivery charges creates a pricing scenario, not proof the old offer can be purchased. Payless labels also reserve price-change discretion; any multi-cycle result is conditional on unchanged charges. A six- or nine-month contract is not projected through twelve cycles without renewal evidence.

We do not measure customer behavior, realized savings, market shares or consumer harm. Findings establish consequences of disclosed pricing rules under specified inputs.

05 / Decision implication

Compare the bill across your usage range.

A plan that ranks well at one disclosure point may perform differently below a credit threshold. Before treating a low rate as a good fit, check billing-cycle variability, the contract horizon and eligibility. This research tool explains those tradeoffs; it does not identify a universally best plan.
