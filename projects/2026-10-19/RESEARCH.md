# Research and role requirements

Reviewed October 19, 2026. Sources below are original government or employer sources. This is a record of research choices, not a claim of job eligibility or company affiliation. No inaccessible LinkedIn content was inferred.

## Airline theme

[BTS delay causes](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/ot_delay/ot_delaycause1.asp) makes recent monthly carrier-airport evidence available. The page/search index showed July 2026, while the actual retrieved export includes August 2026. The file contents, full date coverage and original archive are pinned; no assumption that an indexed page is the latest release. [BTS reporting guidance](https://www.bts.gov/topics/airlines-and-airports/understanding-reporting-causes-flight-delays-and-cancellations), dated April 15, 2024, distinguishes carrier causes from weather, national-airspace and late-aircraft effects. That distinction motivates a narrow carrier-delay investigation screen. It does not identify avoidable minutes or estimate an intervention effect.

## Bank theme

[FDIC Q2 2026 QBP](https://www.fdic.gov/quarterly-banking-profile/quarterly-banking-profile-2q-2026) and the [August 25, 2026 release](https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2026/fdic-insured-institutions-reported-return-assets-137-percent-and-net) describe continuing banking/deposit growth. Search retrieval was available, but direct web-page opening returned 403. The empirical analysis relies on the successfully retrieved [FDIC API](https://api.fdic.gov/banks/docs/) and its preserved official field dictionary, not scraped QBP tables. The latest API universe independently contains 4,238 insured domestic-bank institutions and $20.722tn in total deposits. Distinguishing deposit-volume growth from funding-rate pressure is the research question. No claim about Q3 2026 industry data availability is made.
