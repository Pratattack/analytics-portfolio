# Candid assessment

This is substantive portfolio research: original monthly operating-carrier/airport data, row-level source lineage, audit/quarantine, SQL peer construction, a frozen comparison cohort, constrained priorities, a later-period diagnostic, dependence-aware sensitivity and conditional pilot economics. It is distinct from the portfolio's historical bike-rental forecasting benchmark.

It remains a research prototype. Peer excess is an observational comparison and may reflect schedules, aircraft, routes, stage lengths, carrier reporting and customer mix. The temporal comparison uses revised contemporary data; historical release timing is missing. The bootstrap has only eight months, the candidate pool excludes sparse markets and the policy's diagnostic coverage may be incomplete. The study does not prove that any delay can be removed or that an investment pays off.

The useful interview skill is defensible judgment: distinguish volume from relative performance, show excluded-self denominators, defend the investigation constraint, expose quarantine sensitivity and identify the operational evidence needed before acting. It does not establish industry employment tenure or production readiness. The static figures were visually inspected; browser acceptance scope and actual CI evidence are documented in VISUAL_REVIEW.md.
