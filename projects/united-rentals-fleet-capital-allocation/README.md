# United Rentals: How much can it return after funding its fleet?

**Research cutoff:** November 2, 2026. **Quote date:** October 30, 2026. **Model status:** Scenario analysis, not company guidance.

## The decision
How much cash can United Rentals distribute after funding its rental fleet, financing obligations and a minimum cash reserve?

## Base-case result
The model estimates **$5,430m** operating cash flow and **$4,336m** net investing outflow in its first forecast period. After opening cash, a $150m minimum reserve and a $500m illustrative dividend budget, the modeled repurchase capacity is approximately **$597m** against an illustrative $1,500m repurchase ceiling.

## Approach
Five-year integrated operating and three-statement forecast; a cash-allocation waterfall; DCF, reverse valuation and sensitivity scenarios; independent Python calculation and SQL historical reconciliation.

## Decision relevance
The binding constraint is the cash needed to maintain and expand the fleet. Under higher investment, near-term repurchases can fall even if projected operating cash improves.

## Caveats
The fleet-cohort model is an approximation, not management's fleet-level forecast. Opening fleet stock is rounded, and terminal value represents about 74.9% of base DCF enterprise value. Do not treat modeled distributions as a board authorization or an investment recommendation.
