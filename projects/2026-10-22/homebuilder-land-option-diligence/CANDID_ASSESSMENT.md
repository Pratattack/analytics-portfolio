# Candid assessment

This is a narrow, original-source contract-boundary and capital-decision research prototype. The defensible contribution is identifying economic versus reported scope, reconciling category changes and refusing sunk-cost reasoning. The option mathematics is intentionally transparent and the probabilities are not estimated. It is smaller and less empirically granular than an actuarial triangle or a deal-level land model. Source rigor does not convert a small aggregate sample into a senior professional engagement.

There is no supported issuer-specific exercise recommendation, estimated option value, causal claim or predictive promotion. Deal-level data, contract enforceability, development costs and stakeholder review are missing.

Actual browser and clean reproducibility checks validate software behavior, not external business adoption. No tenure, employment relationship, live hosting or guaranteed recruiter outcome is claimed.
