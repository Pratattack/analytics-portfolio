# Open evidence issues

- Aggregate contract portfolios lack tract-level values, project budgets and individual strike/expiry schedules.
- Refundability and extension rights are unobserved; a legacy taxonomy name cannot establish them.
- MTH 2021 committed subtotal includes recorded scope; historical mapping uses the three excluded-recorded components.
- PHM deposit/pre-acquisition, MTH cash and KBH cash-plus-separate-cost bases are not fully comparable.
- Current observation dates differ (June and August 2026). Newer than the last annual filing does not mean current treasury data.
- Recorded specific-performance contracts are outside the optional portfolio. Do not add their asset values as hypothetical cancellable prices.
- Browser acceptance does not cover physical devices, assistive screen readers or stakeholder approval.
