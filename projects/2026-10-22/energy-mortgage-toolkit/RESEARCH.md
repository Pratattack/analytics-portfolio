## Mortgage origination: comparability before lender selection

The FFIEC's [June 23, 2026 release](https://www.ffiec.gov/news/press-releases/2026/an-06-23) documents the public release of 2025 mortgage data. The [Data Browser API](https://ffiec.cfpb.gov/documentation/api/data-browser/) supplies official filtered loans, a separate aggregation, and reporting-institution names. The [public LAR dictionary](https://ffiec.cfpb.gov/documentation/publications/loan-level-datasets/lar-data-fields) defines product, pricing, public rounding, missing and exempt fields. The empirical core is the API response actually collected October 22, not a reconstruction of a borrower's 2025 information set. Do not combine the March preliminary modified-LAR announcement with the later snapshot as if they were identical vintages. A release announcement does not prove the exact as-served bytes were frozen on that release date.

The substantive question is whether a useful, auditable common population can support historical lender pricing comparisons once product, loan size, CLTV, broad DTI and county are aligned. APR minus its date-matched APOR is more informative than comparing raw note rates across an entire year, but it does not identify a causal lender premium. The result supports a comparable-quote workflow rather than an automated lender ranking. The CFPB's [Loan Estimate explanation](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/loan-estimate/) motivates comparing actual offers and rate/fee terms. The separate amortization workbench makes the holding-period/fee tradeoff explicit.

## Upstream exploration and production: book liability is not a retirement budget

The source manifest lists 17 original SEC 10-K documents, their report periods, actual filing dates, URLs and hashes. The latest is 2025 accounting information filed in February 2026; no future filings or reconstructed point-in-time trading signals are used. Selection covers six upstream issuers with different asset mixes. It is purposive, not a representative sample of all oil and gas operators.

The [COP 2025 annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1163165/000116316526000009/cop-20251231.htm) explains retirement obligations and uncertainty around timing, costs and discounting. The original ledger distinguishes spending from property dispositions. This is the key business risk in using a generic XBRL liabilities-settled tag as a budget input. Our independent table audit found presentation signs that differ from normalized taxonomy signs; both are retained. Conditional optimization then shows exactly what additional timing assumptions would be needed before translating book PV into a common-rate cash equivalent. It does not assert that these assumptions describe an issuer's actual portfolio.

Only confirmed profile skills (Python, SQL, economics and business analysis) are positioned as existing strengths.
