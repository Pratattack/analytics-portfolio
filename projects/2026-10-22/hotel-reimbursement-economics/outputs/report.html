Decision in one minute

Across three operators, H1 2026 cost-recovery revenue is $13.926B of $20.784B reported revenue; the net reimbursement balance is $-0.326B.

Use the exact income bridge before authorizing program spending. Obtain contractual recoverability and a forward operating budget; neither reimbursement revenue nor normalized operating income is free cash.

Where the conclusion stops

The 216 scenarios vary assumed reimbursement growth, cost growth, incremental program spend and a management income floor. They hold the non-reimbursement business fixed, so they are local budget sensitivities rather than earnings forecasts. Excluding net reimbursement balances does not remove owned-hotel activity, corporate costs, co-brand revenue or other GAAP items. All six within-issuer period-matched year-over-year income changes reconcile exactly to the core and reimbursement components. Historical comparative disclosures are selected as available at the cutoff, not recreated as originally known at each date.

Information cutoff: filings through 21 October 2026; latest observations are explicitly dated. Public numerical facts and original source links are pinned. Adoption gate remains false because decision-critical contractual/forward inputs are absent. No causal claim or professional tenure is implied.
