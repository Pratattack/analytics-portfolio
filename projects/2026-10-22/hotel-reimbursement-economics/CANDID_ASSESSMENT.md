# Candid assessment

This is a narrow original-source accounting and program-budget research prototype. The contribution is context-specific revenue/expense mapping, exact reconciliation, period discipline and the budget feasibility boundary. It is not a complex predictive model, a pure-fee profitability valuation or an estimate of cash collection. Twenty-two selected annual records do not support causal or probabilistic industry claims.

Program contracts, actual forward costs, acquisition adjustments, owner recoverability and independent finance stakeholder review remain missing. A mathematically correct bridge does not authorize a spending or investment decision.

Actual browser and clean reproducibility checks validate software behavior, not external business adoption. No tenure, employment relationship, live hosting or guaranteed recruiter outcome is claimed.
