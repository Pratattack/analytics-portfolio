# Open evidence issues

- CHH 2018/2019 reimbursement mappings are not filled from dissimilar historical revenue categories; inspect exclusions.
- Annual history is disclosed in later source vintages, not reconstructed real-time data.
- Acquisitions, co-brand fees, owned hotels and retained accounting items confound pure fee comparisons.
- Cost reimbursement balances can reflect timing, recoverability or program spending; this dataset does not identify causes or cash settlement.
- Historical gains/impairments outside a cost subtotal require explicit statement bridges.
- Actual recovery clauses, budgets, service levels and cash collection timing are unobserved.
- Browser acceptance does not cover physical devices, assistive screen readers or stakeholder approval.
