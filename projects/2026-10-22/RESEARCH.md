## Why these two industries

The repository already covers chemicals, credit cards, utilities, outpatient operations, mobility, airlines, banking, insurance, retail REITs, semiconductor equipment and freight rail. These cases add residential homebuilding and hotel management/franchising with distinct methods: a contract-boundary register plus contingent exercise analysis, and an exact income normalization plus budget feasibility frontier. They do not recycle a forecasting benchmark or cash-payout review.

D.R. Horton's [June 2026 quarterly filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/882184/000088218426000096/dhi-20260630.htm) describes continuing incentives and inventory/land-option review. It informed the theme; it is not part of the three-issuer analytic sample. Meritage, Pulte and KB source documents and observation dates are pinned in the individual manifests. No mortgage elasticity or observed option value is inferred.

Marriott's [August 3, 2026 results](https://marriott.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/marriott-international-reports-second-quarter-2026-results) distinguish reimbursement revenue, related expense and adjusted results. This supports a reimbursement boundary question; actual project observations come from original SEC filings, not a press-release aggregate alone.

## Employer requirements checked

[Lennar Financial Analyst](https://careers.lennar.com/job/1966/financial_analyst?change_lang=en), inspected October 22, emphasizes accurate financial reporting, data extraction/modeling, land budgets, underwriting pro formas, financial feasibility and communicating recommendations. The land case demonstrates a subset of these tasks through Python, SQL and executive explanation; it does not imply Lennar sponsorship or that the role is entry-level.

[Marriott Senior Revenue Analyst](https://careers.marriott.com/senior-revenue-analyst/job/P1-7362822-0), inspected October 22, emphasizes analysis of revenue/profit/demand, budgets, forecasts and sales-strategy diagnostics. The hotel case addresses financial definition and budget decision support, not room-level demand forecasting. [Marriott Financial Analysis & Strategic Planning](https://careers.marriott.com/manager-financial-analysis-strategic-planning-apec/job/P1-6330988-0) describes financial-statement interpretation and actionable leadership recommendations; it is a capability reference, not a claim to management tenure. Job pages may change or close.

The project uses confirmed Python/SQL and Excel-compatible CSVs with plain executive documents. It does not claim Power BI, Tableau or inaccessible LinkedIn contents. Private resume/contact information is excluded.

## Source reuse and limits

[SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) explains entity-wide API constraints; issuer-specific contracts and reimbursement boundaries require original inline contexts. Only numerical fact projections are redistributed, with original URLs/hashes. This avoids publishing complete issuer narratives and preserves traceability. No complete prospective-vintage archive, contractual verification or industry-representative sampling is claimed.
