# Executive brief: do not turn book ARO directly into a cash budget

**Decision owner:** upstream corporate finance / acquisition diligence. **Decision:** can consolidated book retirement obligations size a retirement reserve? **Recommendation:** withhold approval until timing, settlement and transfer evidence are reconciled at asset level.

## Measured evidence

| Evidence | Result | Decision implication |
|---|---:|---|
| Original annual filings | 17 across six issuers | Source accession and actual filing date pinned |
| Reconciled issuer-year ledgers | 35, reconstructed from 257 operands | Every retained opening-plus-movements ledger closes |
| Latest observed book ARO | $15.274867b, December 31, 2025 | Consolidated historical accounting stock, not 2026 cash requirement |
| Explicit cash-paid observations | Six, all COP | Other removals are not silently relabeled cash |
| COP 2025 cash spending / property dispositions | $259m / $186m | Generic settled-liability tags do not identify cash payments |
| Conditional timing cases | 216, including 18 infeasible | Infeasibility concerns assumptions, not issuer solvency |

With a hypothetical common nominal rate 6%, PV-weighted mean year 15 and maximum first-five-year PV share 25%, the six-issuer sum has a conditional cash-equivalent range of **$36.607108b–$48.513173b**. This is a sharp interval under those assumptions, not a forecast. COP's illustrative range is $20.044813b–$26.564171b versus $8.364b book. A single book stock cannot tell treasury when or how much cash will be needed.

## Diligence action list

Request an asset-level retirement calendar, engineering cost estimates, original discount layers, payment evidence, divestiture assumption/indemnity agreements and legal-recovery information. Reconcile mixed settlements-and-divestitures categories before importing them into a liquidity model. Treat the stock-to-cash conversion as a diagnostic until these controls pass. No reserve recommendation, issuer credit rating or measured cost saving follows from this prototype.

## What Prathamesh should understand

A retirement obligation is an accounting estimate of future work such as plugging wells and removing equipment. It is recorded at a discounted value. It can change because new assets are added, estimates change, time passes, assets are sold, currencies move or work is performed. A line that removes a liability may reflect a sale rather than a cash payment. The project demonstrates why the original table and its scope matter more than a convenient standardized tag.

The timing tool asks a conditional question: if a fixed book value were discounted at one assumed rate and retirement timing met specified limits, what cash totals would be mathematically possible? The bounds are exact for that model, but the model does not reveal the issuer's actual schedule. In an interview, distinguish mathematical correctness from economic identification, explain the impossible mean-year/near-term-cap combination, and name the operational evidence needed before approving a reserve.
