# Candid assessment

**Passing source-audit and decision-diagnostic prototype; reserve approval withheld.**

The main original contribution is the demonstrated accounting-data trap: displayed ledger signs and settlement/divestiture semantics can differ from normalized XBRL expectations. We reconstructed complete disclosure ledgers rather than using a loose collection of similarly named facts. The hand checks, exact source operands, original hashes and a clean build make that claim auditable. The conditional optimization is independently verified through a different formulation, exposes infeasible assumptions, and displays attaining profiles.

The empirical panel is 35 irregular issuer-years from 17 filings, not a large representative industry sample or continuous history. It lacks well-level costs, dates, original discount layers and legal recoveries. Book values are affected by acquisitions and asset mix. Only COP provides an explicitly tagged cash-paid series in this extraction; other firms' cash payments are unknown here, not zero. We deliberately avoid a cash-spend ranking.

The bounds are sharp only for the stated common-rate,30-year,PV-mean/cap model. Holding book PV fixed while varying a common rate does not remeasure the GAAP obligation; it is a conditional sensitivity convention. A large modeled dollar range is not an empirical forecast or measured liability understatement. Six issuers having the same assumed constraints is illustrative. There is no operational evidence that the assumed mean/cap is correct.

This legitimately ends with a withheld funding decision. A production reserve tool requires engineering schedules, cash invoices, legal transfer detail and treasury constraints. Adding unsupported discount rates or synthetic well counts would make the estimate look precise while weakening the evidence. CI and browser acceptance verify the implemented workflow, not the missing economic inputs. Independent portfolio work does not establish engineering, actuarial or employment tenure credentials.
