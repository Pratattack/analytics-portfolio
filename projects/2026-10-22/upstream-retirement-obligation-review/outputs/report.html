Historical public data and explicit decision assumptions · filings through 2026-10-21; collected 2026-10-22

Decision brief

Six issuers disclose $15.275B in 2025 book retirement obligations. All 35 retained issuer-year ledgers reconcile. Only COP supplies an explicitly tagged cash-paid series in this extraction; other liability removals cannot be relabeled cash spending.

Request asset-level timing, original discount layers, settlement invoices and divestiture transfers before using book ARO to size a reserve. The timing workbench produces tight conditional bounds and exposes infeasible assumptions; it does not estimate actual retirement dates.

What would change the decision?

The panel samples annual disclosures from 2019, 2022 and 2025 filings and their comparable ledger years; it is not a continuous history. APA uses the current parent only. No asset-level quantities, inflation paths, legal transfer recoveries or issuer-specific discount curves are observed. A common nominal rate is a scenario convention, not GAAP remeasurement.
