Interpretation limits

Public HMDA omits usable individual credit scores, exact lock dates, negotiated concessions and borrower shopping alternatives. APOR adjusts the rate-spread benchmark for pricing date but cannot remove selection. Costs are gross, not net of lender credits. Loan amounts are privacy rounded. Common support excludes most selected-lender loans; perturbation bands measure descriptive stability, not population sampling or causal uncertainty.
