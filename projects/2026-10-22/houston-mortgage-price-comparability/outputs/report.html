Decision brief

Of 123,445 originated loans, 32,095 meet the product and completeness contract. Five lenders contribute 9,344 eligible loans, but only 3,867 remain in 18 common cells. Lender APR-spread range falls from 2.001 to 1.902 percentage points after standardization.

Use the evidence to define comparable product/risk buckets, then request contemporaneous Loan Estimates and compare rates, fees and exit balances. Do not choose a lender or infer discrimination from this historical table. A lender affiliated with a builder may bundle incentives that these public fields cannot separate.

What would change the decision?

Public HMDA omits usable individual credit scores, exact lock dates, negotiated concessions and borrower shopping alternatives. APOR adjusts the rate-spread benchmark for pricing date but cannot remove selection. Costs are gross, not net of lender credits. Loan amounts are privacy rounded. Common support excludes most selected-lender loans; perturbation bands measure descriptive stability, not population sampling or causal uncertainty.
