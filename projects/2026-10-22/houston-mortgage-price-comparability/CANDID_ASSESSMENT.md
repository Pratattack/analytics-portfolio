# Candid assessment

**Passing research prototype; automatic lender recommendation withheld.**

The empirical core is a complete official Houston-originations query, not a small public benchmark relabeled as business research. Row/field lineage, independently reconciled official aggregate, explicit vintage, count-based lender selection, fixed overlap target, tract dependence, sensitivity and an amortization decision workflow are implemented. The original47MB response was audited against every retained projected field. Pinned input projection makes clean offline reproduction possible without silently accepting future HMDA revisions.

The central result is partly negative: five-lender overlap retains 41.38% of selected eligible loans, and a large price gap survives observed matching. That does not establish a causal lender advantage. Credit score, exact public lock dates, underwriting, negotiated/bundled incentives and borrower selection are missing; coarse bins do not balance exact within-bin values. `covariate_balance.csv` makes this visible. Credit/points disclosure availability is audited separately. Positive cluster multipliers measure composition stability, not a calibrated confidence interval for a lender effect. Closer ordering changes under stricter support.

A larger regression or more test cases would not cure identification. Next work should secure contemporaneous offers and channel/credit-score information with suitable permission, then predefine a richer product/borrower design. The calculator is a useful assumption-driven workflow but cannot create real offers or measure actual savings.

Three analytical/reproduction stages and actual browser acceptance are required for release. Verification records identify their scope; browser screenshots/CI are evidence of executed checks, not a claim of comprehensive accessibility certification or production reliability. The work demonstrates independent financial analysis, data engineering and decision communication; it is not equivalent to years of employment or an underwriting qualification.
