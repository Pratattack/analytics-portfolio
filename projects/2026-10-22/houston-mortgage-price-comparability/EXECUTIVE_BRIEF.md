# Executive brief: compare the borrower population before comparing lenders

**Decision owner:** mortgage research / consumer-finance strategy analyst. **Decision:** what evidence should inform a comparable-quote shortlist? **Recommendation:** use matched historical pricing as a research diagnostic, then collect actual contemporaneous Loan Estimates. Withhold automatic lender selection.

## Measured findings

| Evidence | Result | Decision implication |
|---|---:|---|
| Official Houston 2025 originated loans | 123,445 | Granular public empirical core; not a synthetic benchmark |
| Eligible comparable-product loans | 32,095 | Product and reporting completeness sharply restrict the question |
| Five largest eligible lenders | 9,344 eligible loans | Freeze cohort using counts, not prices |
| All-five common-support target | 18 cells; 3,867 loans | Only 41.38% retained; extrapolation is substantial |
| Unmatched spread range | 2.001 percentage points | Annual lender means mix unlike populations |
| Matched spread range | 1.902 percentage points | Observed matching explains little of the overall gap; it does not prove a lender effect |
| Tract-weight stability | 400 perturbations; 1,044 clusters | Descriptive stability conditional on frozen support |

Matched APR-minus-APOR means (percentage points) are approximately United Shore 0.160, Rocket 0.049, JPMorgan Chase −0.082, Lennar Mortgage −1.742, CMG 0.113. These are not offers available to an individual. In particular, bundled incentives, credit scores, origination channels and unobserved shopping behavior can produce apparent pricing gaps. The risk-adjustment label should be limited to the specific public bands actually matched.

The min-cell sensitivity retains 35/18/5 base cells at thresholds 5/10/20. Some close lender ordering changes, while the broad overlap problem persists. The fee diagnostic is gross total loan costs per rounded loan dollar; missing lender credits are not zero and APR is not added to fees.

## Workflow and decision constraints

Use the cell table to check whether a proposed borrower population exists across all five lenders. Obtain dated offers for the same principal/product and ask for points, credits and concessions. Enter hypothetical note rates, upfront fees and a realistic holding period into the offline workbench. It includes remaining principal at exit, which a payment-only comparison misses. Gather sensitivity to holding period and financing plans before making a recommendation. No benefit or savings from an actual borrower decision is measured.

## What Prathamesh should understand

This project asks whether lender prices can be compared fairly using public data. Many loans look different before the analysis even reaches prices, so the first job is to define the same type of loan and find borrower groups represented at every lender. The matched target contains fewer than half the selected lenders' eligible loans. That is a substantive business limitation, not a reason to quietly broaden the model until every loan is included.

The remaining price differences are descriptive. Public data do not show everything lenders knew or every concession a borrower received. In an interview, explain why APR spread is a better annual comparison than raw note rates, why matching cannot remove hidden selection, and why mortgage shopping should ultimately use actual offers. The workbench's rate-versus-fee comparison also depends on how long a borrower keeps the loan and on the balance still owed at exit.
