# CFO Advisory Memo — Preliminary
**Decision:** Evaluate working capital efficiency at Apple relative to Dell and Microsoft. This is a portfolio simulation, not client advice.

Apple FY2025: DSO ~32.09, DIO ~10.74, DPO ~114.66, CCC ~-71.82 days. Dell FY2026 CCC ~-30.14; Microsoft FY2026 CCC ~-33.40. Different operating models and fiscal years make direct targets inappropriate.

**Illustrative scenarios:** Conservative ~$873M, base ~$2.44B, stretch ~$4.67B theoretical gross cash release. Base assumes DSO -2 days, DIO -1 day, DPO +1 day and 70% realization. These are not verified achievable savings or incremental profit.

**Recommendation:** Before implementing any policy, reconcile every source figure to signed 10-K/XBRL facts, test quarterly seasonality, analyze supplier discounts and concentration, segment trade vs vendor receivables, and quantify implementation costs. No causal effects are estimated.
